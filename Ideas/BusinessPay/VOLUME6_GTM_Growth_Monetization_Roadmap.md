# Volume 6: GTM, Growth & Monetization — BusinessPay

## 1. Business Model & Monetization Tiers
- **SaaS Subscription Tier**: Monthly fee per active AR collector / seat ($49 - $199/month).
- **Accelerated Liquidity Take-Rate**: 0.25% – 0.50% processing fee on accelerated early payment cash flow routed through the platform.
- **Enterprise Custom Connectors**: One-time integration fee for legacy ERP connectors (SAP, Oracle ERP Cloud).

## 2. Go-To-Market Strategy
- **ERP App Stores**: Distribution via Tally App Marketplace, Zoho Marketplace, and SAP Store.
- **CFO & Controller Outreach**: Direct outbound targeting of Finance Directors at mid-market B2B distributors and manufacturers.
- **Financial Advisor & Accounting Firm Partnerships**: Referral agreements with accounting practices managing AR for multiple SMB clients.
