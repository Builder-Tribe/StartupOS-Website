# Volume 1: Vision, Problem & Market Opportunity — BusinessPay

## 1. Executive Summary
BusinessPay is an AI-powered B2B Accounts Receivable (AR) management and cash acceleration platform. It empowers finance teams, AR collectors, and CFOs to reduce Days Sales Outstanding (DSO), resolve customer disputes, automate payment commitments, and unlock early-payment liquidity without expensive debt factoring.

## 2. Problem Statement
- **Ballooning DSO**: B2B companies across India and emerging markets suffer from average payment delays of 60–90 days past terms.
- **Manual Collection Fatigue**: AR teams spend 70% of their time on manual follow-ups, spreadsheet tracking, and reactive dispute handling.
- **Inelastic Liquidity**: Supply chain financing and invoice factoring charge high interest rates (18–24% APR) while requiring tedious paper documentation.
- **Dispute Friction**: Unresolved pricing or quantity disputes stall total invoice payment until long after resolution.

## 3. Market Size & Opportunity (TAM / SAM / SOM)
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market)**: $24B Global B2B Accounts Receivable Automation Software.
- **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)**: $3.2B Mid-market & Enterprise B2B SaaS & Manufacturing sector in South Asia & APAC.
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)**: $180M initial focus on Indian mid-market enterprises utilizing Tally, Zoho Books, SAP, and custom ERPs.
