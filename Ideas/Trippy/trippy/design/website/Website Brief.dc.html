07 — Conversion flows

Dual-audience journeys

Two annotated flows. Every step has a design annotation explaining the decision.

Flow A — Solo Traveller
1
Lands on homepage
Via Instagram ad, friend referral, or organic search. Mobile browser, 90% likelihood.
⚑ Hero headline must land in <2s. No loading state visible above fold.
2
Reads hero headline + sees badges
"Going solo doesn't mean going alone." lands emotionally. Sees App Store + Play Store badges immediately — both above fold.
⚑ Badges must be styled as buttons, not images. Tap target ≥44px.
3
Scrolls — sees proof + "How it works"
Stats strip validates scale (12,400+ travellers). Step cards show the path is short: 3 steps.
⚑ AI match score pill visible in step 2 card — seeds the concept before the product section.
4
Reads testimonials — identifies with Arjun/Sneha/Rohan
Stories are demographically matched: 24–28, Indian cities, real destinations. They see themselves.
⚑ Testimonials must feel specific, not generic. First name + city + destination + score.
5
Scrolls past partner section (doesn't apply)
Partner section is visually contained inside a dark card — clearly a different audience. They scroll past without friction.
⚑ Partner section must not confuse travellers or distract from the primary flow.
6
Reaches final CTA — taps App Store or Play Store
Second appearance of both badges. "Free · India-first · Android & iOS" removes final friction objections.
✓ Conversion: app download initiated.
Flow B — Travel Operator / Partner
1
Lands on homepage
Via word of mouth from another operator, or searches "list group trips India platform". Likely desktop.
⚑ Nav must show "For partners" link. Faint color — discoverable but not aggressive.
2
Skims hero — doesn't self-identify as traveller
Hero copy is clearly traveller-facing. Operator sees "this is for travellers" and continues scrolling to find their entry point.
⚑ Partner section must be findable within 2 scrolls. No more than 4 sections above it.
3
Notices partner section — headline resonates
"Running group trips? You're missing 12,000+ solo travellers actively looking." lands precisely for an operator who runs trips but lacks reach.
⚑ Headline must be operator-perspective, not platform-perspective. "You're missing" not "We have".
4
Clicks "Learn more about the Partner CRM"
Secondary link below primary CTA routes to /for-partners — the full partner pitch page.
⚑ Two CTA options: "List your first trip free" (fast track) vs "Learn more" (considered). Both needed.
5
Reads /for-partners — objections addressed
FAQ section removes "do you take commission?" and "do I need to integrate payments?" objections. Operator stories provide peer validation.
⚑ FAQ must answer the top 4 real objections, not generic ones.
6
Clicks "Create your partner account" → /partner
Routes to the Partner CRM signup. Separate app, separate design language (opaque, professional).
✓ Conversion: partner signup initiated at /partner.
08 — Micro-interactions

5 moments of delight

Each micro-interaction must feel earned — it marks a meaningful moment in the traveller journey, not a decoration.

Trigger
Compatibility score reveal
When a match card loads or enters viewport, the score pill animates from 0% → actual score (e.g. 84%) over 600ms with an ease-out curve. The pill color transitions simultaneously: gray → teal (high) or amber (mid).
The score is the product's core differentiator. Animating it in draws the eye, makes the number feel earned rather than arbitrary, and communicates "the AI just calculated this for you."
Trigger
Travel request sent ripple
When a user taps "Send travel request", the button immediately transforms to "Request sent ✓" (optimistic UI, 0ms delay). A subtle ripple pulse radiates from the button outward — 2 expanding rings, teal, 300ms ease-out, opacity 0→0. No spinner.
Sending a travel request is the highest-stakes action in the app. Instant visual feedback + the ripple signal "you've done something real." The pulse mirrors a heartbeat — this matters.
Trigger
Hostel member count tick-up
When the hostel member list loads, the member count badge animates from 0 → actual number (e.g. "6 members") with a fast count-up at 40ms/digit. Each new member row slides in from the right with 80ms stagger between rows.
Shows the hostel as a living, dynamic space rather than a static list. "6 members are already there" feels exciting; seeing the number tick up to 6 feels even more so. Mirrors the social energy of arriving at a hostel.
Trigger
Match notification ripple (nav badge)
When a new match arrives (or an incoming travel request), the Matches nav icon gets a badge with a pulsing ring: the badge scales from 1.0 → 1.3 → 1.0 in a 1.5s loop. Ring color: teal. Badge disappears when the tab is opened.
A new match is the equivalent of someone tapping you on the shoulder. The pulsing ring creates urgency without aggression — it's warm, not alarming. Uses the same visual language as a heartbeat or a notification from a person, not a system.
Trigger
DIY path step completion celebration
When a user completes a DIY step (e.g. hostel selected, or group formed), the step number in the stepper changes from coral → teal, and a checkmark icon pops in with a scale(0) → scale(1.2) → scale(1.0) spring at 250ms. A soft confetti burst (6 particles, coral + teal, 400ms) emits from the step dot.
The DIY path is a multi-step flow that can feel long. Celebrating each completed step reinforces progress and creates a sense of momentum. The coral → teal color shift signals "you built this, and it's becoming real." The confetti is small enough to feel warm, not game-y.
