# Volume 3: Creator & Media Kit Management System — Business/Vendor PRD — CollabKaro

## 1. Creator Media Kit & Portfolio Builder
- **Social Media Verification**: Verify Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn handles.
- **Audience Demographics**: Top cities, age/gender breakdown, average views per reel, and engagement rate metrics.
- **Rate Card Configurator**: Define standardized pricing for Instagram Reels, Stories, Carousels, YouTube Dedicated/Integrated videos, and UGC Ad Rights.

## 2. Application & Content Submission Portal
- **Inbound Brief Discovery**: Browse active brand campaigns matching creator profile & niche.
- **Proposal Submission**: Pitch creative concepts, upload draft scripts, and submit custom quotes.
- **Asset Upload & Proof of Delivery**: Upload high-res video drafts, paste live post URLs, and submit analytics screenshots for milestone release.
