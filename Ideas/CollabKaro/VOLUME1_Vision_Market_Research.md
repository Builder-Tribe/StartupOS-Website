# Volume 1: Vision, Problem & Market Opportunity — CollabKaro

## 1. Executive Summary
CollabKaro is an India-first, two-sided marketplace and operating system for brands, agencies, influencers, UGC creators, affiliate partners, and offline event creators. It governs the full collaboration lifecycle:
`discover → attract → qualify → negotiate → contract → fund → create → approve → publish/deliver → measure → pay → retain`.

## 2. Target Users & Jobs-to-be-Done
- **Brand Owner / Marketer**: Discover creators meeting precise campaign specs, contract them, control budget, and track ROI.
- **Agency**: Manage multi-brand client campaigns, source creators, and report campaign performance.
- **Creator / Influencer**: Media kit presentation, receive inbound campaign briefs, negotiate fair terms, deliver assets, get paid via escrow.
- **UGC Creator**: Sell ad-ready video/photo assets without requiring a massive follower audience.
- **Offline Creator**: Participate in store launches, event activations, and campus ambassador programs.
- **Administrator**: Moderate listings, verify creator metrics, govern escrow disputes, and enforce platform safety.

## 3. Market Size & Opportunity (TAM / SAM / SOM)
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market)**: $21B Global Creator Economy & Influencer Marketing Software Market.
- **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)**: $1.8B Indian Influencer Marketing & UGC Content Market.
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)**: $120M initial focus on D2C brands, e-commerce sellers, and regional creators across Tier 1 & Tier 2 cities in India.
