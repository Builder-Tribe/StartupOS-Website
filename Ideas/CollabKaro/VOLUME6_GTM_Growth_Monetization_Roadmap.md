# Volume 6: GTM, Growth & Monetization — CollabKaro

## 1. Business Model & Commission Tiers
- **Marketplace Commission**: 10% platform fee on brand campaign spend + 5% payout fee on creator earnings.
- **SaaS Agency Tier**: Monthly subscription (₹4,999 - ₹19,999/month) for agencies running multi-brand campaigns.
- **Escrow Interest & Transaction Fees**: Revenue share on payment gateway processing.

## 2. Go-To-Market Channels
- **D2C Brand Partnership Networks**: Direct partnerships with Shopify/WooCommerce D2C merchant communities in India.
- **Creator Onboarding Bootcamps**: Campus ambassador programs and UGC creator training academies.
