# Volume 2: Brand & Agency Campaign Portal PRD — CollabKaro

## 1. Brand Campaign Management Hub
- **Campaign Brief Builder**: Title, Brand Name, Product Category (*Fashion*, *Beauty*, *Tech*, *Fitness*, *Food*), Deliverables (*Instagram Reel*, *YouTube Short*, *UGC Ad Asset*, *Event Appearance*), Target Budget, Application Deadline.
- **Creator Shortlisting & Matching**: Filter creators by niche, engagement rate, follower count, city, language, and media kit verification score.
- **Proposal Review & Negotiation**: Inspect creator proposals, sample work, price quotes, and negotiate revisions.
- **Milestone Escrow Funding**: Fund campaign milestone payments into escrow before creator begins content production.
